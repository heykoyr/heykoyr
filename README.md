## 🚩 SwiftCart — a nationwide delivery marketplace, and the platform that runs it

Customers order from nearby restaurants, pharmacies and supermarkets in one app; independent, licensed local delivery companies fulfil every order with their own riders and their own payment accounts.

The interesting part is the model. Running deliveries nationwide normally means one company holding all the licences and payment infrastructure — a wall for a solo founder. So SwiftCart turns the wall into the business: the companies that **already** hold those licences become the fulfilment layer, and SwiftCart licenses them the consumer brand, the marketplace and the software. A licensing blocker became a SaaS moat.

It's a **four-sided platform I designed and built solo** — a customer app, a vendor app, a rider app, an admin app (one app that's both a company console and a super-admin console), a multi-tenant backend, and the marketing site.

## What I do
